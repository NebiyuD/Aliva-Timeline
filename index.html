Core Strategy

Run two parallel tracks from day one: a short, controlled clinical pilot track that proves value and safety in weeks, and a longer procurement track that matures into contracts over months. The sweet spot is read-only, evidence-linked, auditable outputs plus a workflow that clinicians want to use. Avoid waiting for perfect integration or selling hospital-wide first.

Critical Success Factors

01

Speed of MVP Stabilization

Lock pilot-grade MVP by end of January. Fact table, hybrid retrieval, and source-linked outputs must be trustworthy.

02

Multiple Parallel Pilots

Run 2-4 shadow pilots simultaneously in February. Generate hard metrics fast: minutes saved, click rates, correction rates.

03

Department-Level Sales

Contract structure lets departments buy without full system-wide committee approval. Simple pricing, value-tied terms.

✗

What Kills "Contracts Before Year End"

Waiting for perfect integration. Trying to sell hospital-wide first. Getting stuck in governance with write-back workflows. Over-engineering before proving value.

✓

What Makes It Work

Selling a workflow clinicians immediately adopt. Citations and measurable time savings. Read-only, auditable outputs. Let integration expand after first contract proves you're real.
