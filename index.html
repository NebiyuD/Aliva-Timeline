Critical Success Factors

01

Contracts Before Pilots Live

Best case means contracts signed + cash collected before pilots are fully live (May is ideal). This makes the milestone "done" early.

02

Read-Only Architecture

Keep the product read-only with no PHI storage. Hospital security review pace dominates timelines - avoid scope creep that slows approvals.

03

Security Review Speed

Even with perfect engineering, real timelines are driven by site IT/security review. Start security questionnaires and BAA work early.

✗

What Kills the Timeline

Waiting for perfect integration. Trying to sell hospital-wide first. Adding write-back workflows that trigger governance. Scope creep beyond read-only. Letting security review slip to late summer.

✓

What Makes It Work

Read-only, auditable outputs. Citations and measurable time savings. Upfront payment clauses in contracts. Parallel security review + Topflight work. Demo-ready sandbox that closes deals.
